01. INTRODUCTION

What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

This Cookie Policy explains how ALLYRICH ("we", "us", or "our") utilizes cookies, local storage objects (localStorage), session storage, and related browser technologies when you visit or interact with our storefront at allyrich.com, genrage.vercel.app, or associated domains.

Cookies are compact data files placed on your computer, tablet, or smartphone by a web server. They allow the website to recognize your browser, persist items in your shopping bag across page reloads, maintain your authenticated account session, and ensure maximum transactional security during checkout.

02. CATEGORIES OF COOKIES WE USE

Classification & Functional Purpose

We adhere strictly to data minimization principles under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023. We categorize all client-side storage technologies as follows:

1. Strictly Necessary & Session Cookies (Always Active)

These items are essential for the storefront to operate securely and reliably. Without these technologies, basic e-commerce functions such as adding oversized garments to your cart, generating Cashfree UPI checkout tokens, or logging in via OTP cannot function.

Storage Key / Name Type Duration Purpose
genrage_cart localStorage Persistent (30 Days) Stores your selected garments, sizes, quantities, and active promo discounts.
genrage_user localStorage Persistent Maintains customer authentication session and saved shipping addresses.
allyrich_cookie_consent localStorage Persistent (1 Year) Remembers your cookie preferences so the banner is not displayed on recurring visits.
cf_session_token Session / Secure Cookie Session PCI-DSS Level 1 payment gateway handshake token generated by Cashfree Payments.

2. Functional & Experience Preferences

These technologies enable enhanced storefront functionality and personalized styling:

  • Wishlist & Saved Items: Remembers products you have bookmarked for quick inspection.
  • UI State & Modal Dismissals: Ensures that size guides, chat modals, and promotional alerts only open when explicitly triggered.

3. Analytics & Telemetry (Anonymized)

We monitor anonymous aggregate technical telemetry (e.g. server response times, HTTP 500 error monitoring, and regional network latency) to ensure our high-definition 3D video showcases stream at 60fps across Indian networks. These metrics contain zero personally identifiable information (PII).

4. Direct Communication & Marketing Opt-In

If you explicitly subscribe to the ALLYRICH Inner Circle or opt-in to WhatsApp drop alerts, your phone number and email are stored securely in encrypted databases. We never install third-party tracking cookies that track you across other independent websites.

04. HOW TO MANAGE & WITHDRAW CONSENT

Your Rights & Browser Controls

You have complete authority over how cookies and local storage objects are handled on your device:

  • Storefront Cookie Consent Banner: You can choose to accept all cookies or restrict to Strictly Necessary cookies upon your first visit. You can re-open and update your choice at any time using the button below.
  • Browser Level Controls: All modern web browsers (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge) allow you to block, filter, or delete cookies. Note that blocking Strictly Necessary cookies will prevent checkout from loading.
