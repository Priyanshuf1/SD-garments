PRIVACY POLICY

DATA PROTECTION • PRIVACY RIGHTS • DPDP ACT 2023 COMPLIANCE

EFFECTIVE DATE: October 2026 • LAST REVISED: CURRENT
COMPLIANCE: DIGITAL PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION ACT, 2023 (INDIA)
PRIVACY COMMITMENT: At ALLEYRICH (operated under SD Garments / ALLEYRICH Apparels, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India), we hold client privacy in the highest regard. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal data we collect, how it is secured, and your explicit rights under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

01. PERSONAL DATA WE COLLECT

When you visit allyrich.com, create an account, save a garment to your wishlist, or purchase architectural streetwear, we collect the following categories of data:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Your full name, mobile telephone number, and email address.
  • Delivery & Billing Data: Doorstep shipping address, flat/house number, building, street, landmark, city, state, postal PIN code, and recipient telephone number.
  • Order & Transaction Details: Products purchased, size selections, order timestamps, payment method used, order totals, invoice numbers, and courier tracking numbers. (Note: We never collect or store full credit/debit card numbers or net banking passwords).
  • Technical & Network Identifiers: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser family, operating system, device screen resolution, access timestamps, and HTTP request headers. These are utilized solely for fraud prevention, server health, and rate-limiting.
  • Preference & Session State: Cloud-synced wishlist items, active cart contents, and recent searches saved in local session storage.

02. LAWFUL GROUNDS & PURPOSES OF PROCESSING

We process your personal data only where we have a lawful basis under Indian law, specifically:

  • Contractual Performance: To process, fabricate, pack, and deliver your orders via our logistics partners; to send real-time dispatch alerts and courier tracking links; and to handle size exchanges or refund requests.
  • Account Authentication: To issue 6-digit cryptographic Instant OTPs to your registered email, verify legitimate login requests, and protect your saved address from unauthorized modification.
  • Customer Care & Concierge: To provide direct support regarding garment sizing, delivery status, and order alterations via WhatsApp, Instagram DM, and email.
  • Platform Security & Fraud Prevention: To detect, mitigate, and block distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, automated checkout scalper bots, and unauthorized API probing.
  • Statutory Tax Compliance: To generate GST-compliant tax invoices and comply with mandatory bookkeeping standards mandated by the Government of India.

03. ZERO DATA MONETIZATION GUARANTEE

OUR PLEDGE: ALLEYRICH will never sell, lease, rent, trade, or monetize your personal details, phone number, or email address to third-party marketing brokers, advertising exchanges, or lead aggregators. Your information is used exclusively to fulfill your ALLEYRICH experience.

04. AUTHORIZED THIRD-PARTY DATA PROCESSORS

To deliver a seamless D2C shopping experience, we share essential data strictly on a need-to-know basis with trusted, industry-certified service providers:

Service Partner Role / Function Data Shared
Cashfree Payments RBI-authorized payment aggregator Order total, invoice ID, client name, mobile, email (for PCI-DSS compliant checkout)
Delhivery Express Logistics Nationwide door delivery & reverse pickup Recipient name, doorstep shipping address, PIN code, contact phone number
Resend API Transactional email dispatcher Customer email address (for Instant OTPs, order invoices & shipping alerts)
AWS RDS & Supabase Cloud Encrypted cloud database infrastructure Hashed customer profiles, encrypted orders, and synchronized wishlist state

All third-party processors are contractually bound to process your personal data with enterprise-grade confidentiality and in strict compliance with applicable data privacy laws.

05. COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE TECHNOLOGIES

We use essential browser cookies and local storage tokens to provide core shopping functionality:

  • Session Persistence: To maintain your active shopping bag (genrage_cart) across page transitions so you do not lose your selections.
  • Wishlist Synchronization: To remember garments you have marked with a heart (genrage_wishlist) and sync them across your mobile and desktop devices upon login.
  • Authentication Tokens: Secure, cryptographically signed tokens (genrage_auth_token) to keep you signed in securely without repeatedly requesting credentials.

We do not deploy intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers or sell browsing history.

06. DATA SECURITY & STORAGE ARCHITECTURE

We implement robust administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to preserve the confidentiality and integrity of your personal information:

  • Transport Layer Security (TLS): All traffic to and from allyrich.com is encrypted using 256-bit TLS 1.3 certificates.
  • Cryptographic Salting & Hashing: Passwords and authentication secrets are salted and hashed using industry-standard cryptographic algorithms before being committed to database storage.
  • Rate Limiting & Anti-Abuse Defenses: Sliding-window rate limiters and brute-force mitigation lockouts safeguard OTP generation and checkout endpoints from abuse.
  • Access Governance: Only authorized senior engineering and fulfillment personnel have role-based access to customer shipment records.

07. YOUR STATUTORY RIGHTS (DPDP ACT 2023)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you are endowed with enforceable statutory rights as a Data Principal:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of personal data being processed about you and the processing activities undertaken.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You have the right to correct inaccurate or misleading personal data, or complete incomplete data via your Customer Account or by contacting us.
  • Right to Erasure: You may request the deletion of your personal data when it is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to statutory retention obligations under Indian tax laws.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register a grievance with our designated Data Protection & Grievance Officer and receive a formal response within statutory timelines.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate an individual who will exercise your privacy rights in the event of death or incapacity.

08. DATA RETENTION PERIOD

We retain your personal information only for as long as is necessary to satisfy the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Order & Invoicing Data: Retained for 7 (seven) financial years to comply with statutory accounting, GST audit, and taxation mandates governed by the Ministry of Finance, Government of India.
  • Subscriber Emails: Retained until you click "Unsubscribe" or formally request removal from our Inner Circle newsletter.
  • Customer Account & Wishlist Data: Retained as long as your account remains active or until you request account closure.
