ARCHITECTURAL FIT ASSURANCE: We understand that luxury streetwear drape is paramount. If you ordered a size that doesn't fit your desired silhouette, ALLEYRICH offers a seamless 7-Day Size Exchange across all standard catalog products.

01. STEP-BY-STEP EXCHANGE PROCESS

1

INITIATE YOUR REQUEST

Email our concierge at care@allyrich.com or message our WhatsApp Support Desk with your Order ID (e.g. #GR-10482) and the desired replacement size.

2

DOORSTEP REVERSE PICKUP

Once approved, our logistics partner (Delhivery Express) will arrange a reverse pickup from your doorstep within 48 to 72 business hours. Place the garment in its original polybag with all tags attached.

3

WAREHOUSE QUALITY INSPECTION

When the returned garment reaches our Mumbai fulfillment center, our QC team inspects the fabric, tags, and condition within 24 working hours of arrival.

4

EXPRESS DISPATCH OF NEW SIZE

Your newly sized garment is packed and dispatched immediately with priority express shipping. You will receive an SMS and email with fresh live tracking details.

02. MANDATORY RETURN CHECKLIST

Before handing your parcel to the courier executive, please verify that your garment complies with our quality requirements:

  • Original Tags Attached: Woven brand neck label and branded hangtags must be intact and untampered.
  • Unworn & Unwashed: Garments must not show any signs of wear, stretching, washing, or dry-cleaning.
  • Original Packaging: Garment must be enclosed inside its protective ALLEYRICH polybag.
  • Zero Scent or Blemish: Free from deodorant stains, makeup smudges, body odors, cologne scents, or pet hair.
Note: Garments failing quality inspection upon arrival at our warehouse will be sent back to the customer, and the exchange request will be voided.

03. WHAT IF THE DESIRED SIZE IS OUT OF STOCK?

Because ALLEYRICH drops are produced in strictly limited quantities, your desired replacement size may occasionally sell out. In such cases, you will be offered your choice of:

  • Immediate 100% Refund: Processed back to your original source payment method (UPI, Card, Bank).
  • Store Credit Wallet: Issued with lifetime validity, allowing you to secure any upcoming limited drop instantly.
  • Alternate Silhouette: Exchange for any other product of equivalent value from our current collection.

04. SELF-SHIPMENT IN NON-SERVICEABLE PIN CODES

In the rare event that your pin code is outside the reverse-pickup coverage network of Delhivery Express, our concierge team will notify you to self-ship the package via Speed Post or any trusted courier:

  • Send the package to our Central Warehouse: ALLEYRICH Fulfillment Hub, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India - 400001.
  • Share a photo of the courier receipt and tracking number with our team at care@allyrich.com.
  • ALLEYRICH will reimburse your shipping costs up to ₹100 directly to your UPI/bank account upon parcel receipt.

05. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How many times can I exchange an order?

We provide 1 (one) complimentary size exchange per order. Any subsequent exchanges on the same order will incur a nominal two-way courier shipping fee of ₹150.

Can I return or exchange Clearance Sale items?

Items purchased under the "CLEARANCE SALE" section with steep markdowns are strictly Final Sale. We do not accept returns on clearance items, although size adjustments can be accommodated if alternate sizes remain in stock.

How long does the entire exchange cycle take?

From initial pickup to delivery of your fresh size, the typical turnaround time is 5 to 7 business days depending on your city location.
