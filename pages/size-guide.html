HOW TO MEASURE YOUR BODY

CHEST / BUST

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level and under your arms.

LENGTH

Measure from the highest point of your shoulder seam down to the bottom hem of the garment.

WAIST

Measure around your natural waistline, where your trousers or pants normally sit comfortably.

INSEAM

Measure from the crotch seam straight down to the ankle hem along the inner leg seam.
