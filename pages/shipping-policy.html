FAST & RELIABLE FULFILLMENT: ALLYRICH partners with India's premier logistics carriers (Delhivery Express, Bluedart, and Xpressbees) to ensure our heavyweight architectural garments arrive safely, securely, and swiftly at your doorstep.

01. DISPATCH & WAREHOUSE PROCESSING

All orders placed on allyrich.com are processed and packed at our Mumbai central fulfillment warehouse:

  • Standard Processing Window: Orders placed before 1:00 PM IST on business days are dispatched within 24 working hours. All other orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 working hours.
  • Drop Days & Festive Drops: During archival drop releases or high-volume seasonal launches, fulfillment may require an additional 24 hours due to bespoke double-tier QC inspection.
  • Operating Schedule: Warehouse dispatch operations operate Monday through Saturday. Orders placed on Sunday or national holidays are dispatched on the subsequent business day.

02. ESTIMATED DELIVERY TIMELINES

Once dispatched from our fulfillment facility, estimated transit times across Indian geographies are as follows:

Destination Region Sample Cities / Hubs Estimated Transit Time
Tier 1 Metro Hubs Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Pune, Ahmedabad 2 to 4 Business Days

03. SHIPPING CHARGES & FREE DELIVERY THRESHOLD

  • Prepaid Orders (UPI, Cards, Netbanking): FREE Express Doorstep Delivery across all serviceable PIN codes in India. Zero hidden handling fees.
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders: A nominal convenience handling fee of ₹99 is applied at checkout. This fee covers courier cash handling, secure cash transit insurance, and manual order verification.

04. REAL-TIME ORDER TRACKING

We provide continuous digital visibility from the moment your garment leaves our fulfillment facility:

  • As soon as your shipment is scanned by the courier, you will receive an automated SMS and Email notification containing your unique Delhivery Air Waybill (AWB) tracking number.
  • You can track your live shipment milestone-by-milestone directly via our internal tracking portal at allyrich.com/track or directly on the Delhivery website.

05. CASH ON DELIVERY (COD) VERIFICATION

To prevent malicious or accidental orders, ALLYRICH maintains a strict order verification protocol for COD shipments:

  • Our automated verification system may send an SMS OTP or WhatsApp prompt to confirm your intention to receive the COD parcel.
  • Unverified COD orders may be placed on temporary hold. If confirmation is not received within 48 hours, the order will be cancelled to release inventory back to the storefront.
  • Please ensure exact cash is ready upon delivery, or scan the courier's official digital UPI QR code at the doorstep.

06. DELIVERY ATTEMPTS & RETURN TO ORIGIN (RTO)

Our courier partners are mandated to make up to 3 (three) consecutive delivery attempts before initiating Return-to-Origin (RTO):

  • The delivery rider will call your registered contact phone number prior to arrival.
  • If you are unavailable, you may coordinate directly with the delivery executive to reschedule for the following business day.
  • If a shipment returns to our warehouse as undeliverable due to an incorrect address, wrong contact number, or refusal to accept without valid cause, subsequent re-dispatch will require payment of fresh courier charges.

07. TAMPER-PROOF PACKAGING NOTICE

IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTICE: Every ALLYRICH garment is dispatched in an official, tamper-evident security polybag with branded adhesive seals. If you notice that the outer parcel is torn, unsealed, or visibly tampered with upon courier handover:
  • DO NOT ACCEPT THE DELIVERY. Instruct the courier executive to mark the parcel as "Damaged/Refused due to outer tampering".
  • Take immediate photos of the damaged outer bag and notify us at care@allyrich.com with your Order ID.
  • We will expedite a fresh replacement immediately without waiting for the courier investigation.

08. INTERNATIONAL SHIPPING ROADMAP

At present, ALLYRICH delivers exclusively across the Republic of India. Cross-border international shipping to North America, Europe, the United Kingdom, and the Middle East is currently in active development. Sign up for our Inner Circle newsletter to receive worldwide launch announcements.
