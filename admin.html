ALLEYRICH Operations & Core Management Guide

Streetwear Enterprise Infrastructure & Asset Pipeline
📊 1. Executive Overview (Dashboard)

Purpose: Real-time mission control for the brand owner. Aggregates live gross revenue, average order value (AOV), fulfillment velocity (Delivered vs Processing), active promo performance, and dynamic category breakdown (Pants, Tees, Vests, Jewelry) with 1-click drilldowns.

👕 2. Products Catalog Management

Purpose: Complete SKU and inventory governance across all 245+ streetwear apparel items. Control instant price updates, stock replenishment alerts, compare-at pricing, image assignment (upload, URL, or Media Gallery), and tag indexing.

🔥 3. Collections & Seasonal Drops

Purpose: Drives the core streetwear "Hype & Capsule Drop" cycle. Allows scheduling new collection launches (e.g. Acid Wash Drop, Cyberpunk Heavyweight, Monolith Lookbook) with custom banner imagery picked directly from your local Brand Media Gallery or uploaded from your computer.

📦 4. Orders & Shipping Fulfillment

Purpose: High-precision dispatch pipeline. In addition to customer name and phone, each order row visually displays the exact product thumbnail, variant size, and quantity ordered so packing staff never make shipping mistakes. Supports real-time status transitions (Processing → Shipped → Delivered), BlueDart/Delhivery AWB tracking, and instant PDF invoice printing.

👥 5. Customers Hub & Loyalty CRM

Purpose: Manages verified streetwear collectors, VIP buyers, order frequency, total lifetime value (LTV), and multi-city geographic reach (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad).
